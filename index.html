Planet Clicker

Tap a planet. Build a fleet. Collapse into a supernova.

How to play

Tap the planet to collect stardust.

Tap power upgrades raise what each tap gives. Fleet upgrades earn stardust every second, even while you are away.

Every 25 of one upgrade doubles its output. Use the x1, x10, x100 and Max buttons to buy in bulk.

Once you earn 1M stardust in a run, a supernova resets your upgrades for a permanent +10% to everything per supernova.

Once you earn enough stardust in one run you can rebirth. It resets everything, including supernovas, for a permanent +50% and unlocks new worlds with their own bonuses.
